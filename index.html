About Me

Hey, I'm Ron! I'm an indie game developer who enjoys creating browser games and turning ideas into playable projects.

I work on different types of games, from multiplayer experiences to smaller arcade-style projects. I'm always learning new things and looking for ways to improve my games.

I also publish games on CrazyGames.

One of my biggest dreams is to create a game that reaches millions of people and brings joy to everyone who plays it. I love watching game development videos on YouTube, learning how games are made and discovering new ideas that inspire me to keep creating.

Two of my biggest inspirations are Dani and the JustPlay.LOL team. Dani was the first person who introduced me to game development, and from the moment I discovered his videos, I was hooked. He made me realise just how exciting creating games could be. The JustPlay.LOL team also inspired me through 1v1.LOL, one of my favourite Unity-based browser games.

One day, I'd love to create my own games and make content that inspires the next generation of developers, just like the creators who inspired me. I want to prove that you can start with an idea, keep learning, and eventually create something that people genuinely love playing.
